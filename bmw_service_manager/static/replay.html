Watch a drive again

Record a drive with Boostbook's Logger tab on the iPhone, share it to this computer, then drop the .bblog.gz file here or click Open drive.

You'll see the car on a 3D map of the terrain, turning, pitching and rolling as it did; its wheels and xDrive at work in an X-ray view; lap times, charts of every value, and a g-g circle. Export it for Google Earth or a spreadsheet.

The sample is the app's simulator: an X1 xDrive18d around Mount Panorama, Bathurst, from a standing start. The file is read in this browser; nothing is uploaded. The map's imagery and terrain come from Esri, OpenTopoMap, OpenStreetMap and AWS's open terrain tiles.
