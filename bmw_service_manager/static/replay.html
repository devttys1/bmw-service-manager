Record a drive with Boostbook's Logger tab on the iPhone, share it to this computer, then drop the .bblog.gz file here.

  • 3D terrainThe car on the map, turning, pitching and rolling as it did
  • X-rayIts wheels and xDrive at work, close up
  • Laps and chartsLap times, every value, a g-g circle
  • ExportGoogle Earth (.kml) or a spreadsheet (.csv)

The sample is the app's simulator: an X1 xDrive18d around Mount Panorama, Bathurst, from a standing start. The file is read in this browser; nothing is uploaded. The map's imagery and terrain come from the NSW, Queensland and Tasmanian governments' aerial photos, Esri, OpenTopoMap, OpenStreetMap and AWS's open terrain tiles.
