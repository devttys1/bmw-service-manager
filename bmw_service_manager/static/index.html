1. Vehicle

Connect an ENET cable, switch the ignition on, then click Find vehicle.

2. Service history

Filled in when the car is found. Stampbook files the history under this car.
Scan with Stampbook On the iPhone or iPad, open Stampbook and choose History > ⋯ > Import from QR code, then point the camera at this code. It carries the records themselves, not a link.

Records are written in this order. BMW tools write the newest record first. Drag a record to move it, or use ↑ and ↓.

3. Write to head unit


      
The head unit's existing history cannot be read back. Erasing it is permanent, so back up your service records on the head unit before writing.

    


    

      

        
Service due (CBS)
          iReset sets one item to 100% and adds one to its service count, in the ECU that owns it: oil and NOx additive in the DME, brake pads in the DSC, everything else in the instrument cluster. Due dates are sent as unchanged. Only the oil reset has been seen to work on a car. A diagnostic reset may mark the cluster's CBS data as manipulated. The cluster's own service menu does not.

4. Read from head unit experimental

      
No known command reads service history back. These read-only probes look for one. They never start a routine or change anything on the car. The DID scan can target any ECU, in case another one keeps a copy of the history.

      

        
      

      
Optional: enter one entry shown in iDrive under Vehicle info → Service history. The scan flags any response containing its date or mileage.
