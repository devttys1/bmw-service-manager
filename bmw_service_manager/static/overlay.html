Video overlay

Your gauges on your video

Drop a video from a GoPro or a phone and the drive Boostbook's Logger recorded at the same time. Speed, RPM, boost, g-forces, the track and lap times go over it, lined up to the frame, and it's saved as a new MP4.

  • Lined up for youA GoPro's own GPS and accelerometer find the moment; anything else lines up by hand in a few clicks
  • Drag the gaugesPut each where you want it, at the size you want
  • What you see is what you getThe export draws every frame the way the preview does
  • Stays hereThe video is read and made in this browser; nothing is uploaded

Exporting needs a browser with WebCodecs: Chrome, Edge or Safari 17 and later. HEVC (H.265) videos, the default on newer GoPros and iPhones, need a computer that can play them.
